Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 01, 2026

App: SwiftFix

Introduction

SwiftFix is an Android application developed by ONOZEK. It helps users correct, improve, and transform text using AI commands.

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information SwiftFix accesses, how it is used, and when information is shared with third-party AI providers, advertising partners, and analytics services.

Accessibility Service
SwiftFix uses Android's Accessibility Service to detect configured text commands, such as "?fix", in editable text fields across supported apps.

When the service is enabled, SwiftFix may inspect text in editable fields to detect these commands.

When a supported command is detected, SwiftFix processes the relevant text to perform the requested action.

The Accessibility Service is essential to the app's main functionality. You can disable it at any time through your Android settings.

Information Sent to AI Providers
SwiftFix allows you to configure AI providers, including:

Google Gemini
OpenAI
Groq
Anthropic Claude
Custom AI providers that support the app's connection format
When you use an AI command, the relevant text and instructions are sent directly from your device to the AI provider you have selected.

SwiftFix does not operate its own server to receive or process this text. The connection is made directly between your device and the selected provider.

The selected provider may process and retain submitted information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

We do not control how third-party providers handle information after receiving it.

API Keys and Local Storage
To connect to AI providers, you may enter your own API keys.

SwiftFix stores provider settings, API keys, custom commands, and related configuration in its local app database on your device.

Your API keys are used to authenticate requests to the AI providers you configure.

ONOZEK does not operate a server that collects these API keys.

You should protect your device and avoid sharing your API keys with other people. Device backups and other device-level access may also affect the security of locally stored information.

Information We Collect
Based on the current app architecture, SwiftFix does not use an ONOZEK-operated server to collect users' messages or AI requests.

The app processes text locally to detect configured commands and sends relevant text directly to the selected AI provider when a transformation is requested.

Third-party AI providers may collect technical information and submitted content under their own policies.

Advertising
SwiftFix shows a small banner ad, and, if you choose to watch one, a rewarded video ad, both served through Google AdMob.

AdMob may use your advertising ID and other technical information to show and measure ads. This data is handled under Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's own data disclosure.

On first launch, SwiftFix asks for your consent to personalized or non-personalized ads through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), as required in the EU, UK, and similar regions. You can change this choice from the app's settings at any time.

Unlocking custom commands by watching a rewarded ad, and the 7-day streak that removes ads permanently, are tracked only on your device. ONOZEK does not see or store your streak or unlock status.

Analytics and Crash Reporting
SwiftFix uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to understand basic, aggregated app usage, such as which screens are opened, and to receive crash reports when the app fails unexpectedly.

This data is tied to an anonymous device identifier, not to your name or the text you type. It helps us fix bugs and improve the app. See Firebase's privacy and security policy.

Permissions
SwiftFix may use the following Android permissions and services:

Internet: To communicate with your selected AI provider, and to load ads and analytics.
Accessibility Service: To detect configured commands and replace text in supported editable fields.
Display over other apps: To show a small processing indicator when enabled.
Foreground Service: To keep the background functionality running while the service is active.
Battery settings: To let you request an exemption that may help the app remain active in the background.
You can manage these permissions through your Android settings.

Sensitive Information
Do not use SwiftFix to process passwords, banking credentials, private authentication codes, or other highly sensitive information.

Text submitted to an AI provider is handled according to that provider's policies.

Third-Party Services
SwiftFix connects to third-party AI services selected by the user, as well as Google AdMob and Firebase as described above.

These services have their own privacy policies and data-handling practices. We encourage users to review the policies of their selected providers.

Children's Privacy
SwiftFix is not specifically designed for children. Anyone using the app must have the legal authority to agree to the applicable terms and must follow the age requirements of the AI services they use.

Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy when SwiftFix's features or data practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
